Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 28 November 2026

How ordering works

Add products to your bag and tap Checkout. Fill in your delivery details and we'll open WhatsApp with your order ready to send. Once you send it, we confirm stock, the delivery estimate and payment in the same chat. Your order is confirmed only when we reply.

Shipping

  • We ship across India.
  • Orders of Rs 499 and above ship free. Below Rs 499, shipping is Rs 49.
  • We dispatch within 1 to 2 working days of confirming your order. Most orders arrive in 3 to 7 working days; remote PIN codes can take longer.
  • We send a tracking link on WhatsApp when your order is dispatched. To check on an order, message us on WhatsApp.

Payment

Pay by UPI or cash on delivery. For UPI, we share our payment details in the WhatsApp chat after confirming your order. We will never ask for your card number, PIN or OTP.

Returns and refunds

  • If a product doesn't suit you, message us within 30 days of delivery, even if it's opened.
  • Share your order details and a photo of the product. We'll arrange a pickup or tell you where to send it.
  • Once we receive it, we refund to your UPI ID or bank account within 7 working days, or send an exchange if you prefer.
  • One return per product per customer.

Damaged or wrong items

If your parcel arrives damaged, leaking or with the wrong product, message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll send a replacement at no cost.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp. If you've already paid, we refund the full amount.

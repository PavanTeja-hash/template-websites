FAQ

Got Questions?

Transparency is the whole point of our brand. Here are straight answers to the common ones.

How do I place an order?
Add products to your bag, tap Checkout and fill in your delivery details. We'll open WhatsApp with your order ready to send. In the same chat we confirm stock, delivery time and payment (UPI or cash on delivery). Nothing is charged on this website.
Which skin types do your products suit?
The range covers normal, oily, dry, combination and sensitive skin, and each product card says who it suits best. If you're unsure, take the skin quiz or message us on WhatsApp.
Should I patch test first?
Yes, with any new skincare product. Apply a little behind your ear or on your inner arm once a day for 2 to 3 days. If you see redness, itching or swelling, don't use it. Vitamin C can tingle slightly on first use, which is normal, but stop if it stings or burns.
Are your products safe during pregnancy?
None of our products contain retinoids, salicylic acid or hydroquinone. Still, please check with your doctor before adding anything new during pregnancy. We'll send the full ingredient list of any product on request.
How long before I see results?
Give any new product at least 3 to 4 weeks of daily use. Dark spots and marks usually take 8 to 12 weeks. Wear sunscreen every day, because sun exposure undoes the work of brightening products quickly.
Do you offer returns and refunds?
Yes. If a product doesn't suit you, message us within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a refund or exchange, even if it's opened. Details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
How much is shipping and how long does it take?
We ship across India. Orders of Rs 499 and above ship free; below that, shipping is Rs 49. Most orders arrive in 3 to 7 working days. We share a tracking link on WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.
How do I pay?
By UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others) or cash on delivery. We share payment details in the WhatsApp chat once your order is confirmed. We will never ask for your card number, PIN or OTP.
Where are your products made?
All DEWBLOOM products are made in India by a licensed cosmetics manufacturer. The full ingredient list, batch number and expiry date are printed on every pack.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information BREWCRAFT Coffee Co. ("BREWCRAFT", "we") collects through www.brewcraft.in and how we use it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What this website stores

  • Your bag. The coffees in your bag are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they're still there when you come back. This never leaves your device. You can clear it by emptying your bag or clearing your browser data.
  • No accounts, no tracking. This website has no log-in, no advertising cookies and no analytics.
  • Fonts. Text fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, as with any website request.

When you order, subscribe or contact us

The checkout and subscription forms do not send anything to us or to a server. They put your order or plan, and the details you enter, into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. Once you send it, or email or message us, we receive those details and use them only to:

  • confirm, roast, pack, ship and deliver your order or subscription, and take payment;
  • message you before subscription deliveries;
  • handle refunds, replacements and questions about your order;
  • keep the records we are required to keep by law (for example, invoices for tax purposes).

We share your name, phone number and address with our courier partner only to deliver your order. We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy. UPI payments are handled by your UPI app and bank; we never ask for your card number, PIN or OTP.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax records, and chat history only as long as we need it to serve you. If you cancel a subscription, we stop using your details for deliveries straight away.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to what we must keep by law. Contact us using the details below.

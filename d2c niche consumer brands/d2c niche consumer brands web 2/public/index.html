FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first bag.

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?
We roast every Tuesday and ship the same day. Orders confirmed by Monday evening go into that week's roast; later orders go into the next one. Most bags arrive 2 to 5 working days after shipping. The roast date is printed on every bag, and coffee tastes best within 4 weeks of it.
Do you sell ground coffee or only whole beans?
Both. Choose whole bean or a grind for South Indian filter, French press, pour over, AeroPress or espresso before you add a coffee to your bag. We grind just before packing. Whole beans stay fresh longer, so grind at home if you can.
How do subscriptions work?
Pick a coffee, grind and frequency above and send the plan to us on WhatsApp. We confirm your first delivery date, then message you before each delivery. You pay per delivery by UPI or cash on delivery, with 20% off and free shipping. To skip a delivery, pause, change your coffee or cancel, just send us a message. There are no fees and no lock-in.
What if I'm not happy with my order?
If your coffee arrives damaged, or you're unhappy with it, message us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll send a replacement bag or give you a full refund. See Shipping & Returns for details.
Do you ship across India?
Yes, to all PIN codes our courier partners serve. Orders of Rs 599 and above ship free; below that, shipping is Rs 59. Remote PIN codes can take a few extra days. Not sure about yours? Ask us on WhatsApp.
What makes single-origin different from blends?
Single-origin beans come from one region, so you taste what makes that place unique: its altitude, soil and climate. Blends combine beans from more than one origin for a balanced, consistent cup. We offer both: single-origins for exploring, and the Wayanad Robusta Blend for a classic South Indian filter coffee.
How do I pay?
By UPI or cash on delivery. Checkout opens WhatsApp with your order filled in. We confirm it there and share UPI details if you chose UPI. We will never ask for your card number, PIN or OTP.

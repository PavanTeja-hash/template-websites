Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal details Sri Chamundi Tours and Travels ("we") collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with them. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Trip details you choose to share: places, dates, number of adults and children, hotel category and any notes, such as food needs or elders travelling.
  • When you book, the details hotels, airlines, railways and visa offices need, such as traveller names, ages and ID or passport details.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How the enquiry form works

The form does not store anything on this website. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your details are put into a WhatsApp message on your own phone or computer. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Why we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a trip plan and price.
  • To book hotels, cabs, tickets, visas and insurance you ask for.
  • To stay in touch with you during your trip.
  • To issue GST invoices and keep records the law requires.

We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing. We send offers only if you ask us to, and you can stop them at any time.

Who we share them with

Only the hotels, transport companies, airlines, railways, visa offices and insurers needed for your booking, and only what they need. We may also share details when the law requires it.

How long we keep them

Enquiries that do not become bookings are deleted within 12 months. Booking and invoice records are kept for 8 years, as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you, or withdraw consent you have given. Write to us or call the office and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

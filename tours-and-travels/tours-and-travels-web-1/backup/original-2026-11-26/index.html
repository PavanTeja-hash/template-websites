Kerala in six days,day by day

This is our most booked trip from Bengaluru. Every package comes with a plan like this, written for your dates.

  1. Day 1, Kochi

    Pickup at Kochi airport or Ernakulam station. Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets and a Kathakali show in the evening.

  2. Day 2, Kochi to Munnar

    Four hour drive past Cheeyappara and Valara falls. Evening at leisure in the tea country.

  3. Day 3, Munnar

    Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty dam, the tea museum and Echo Point.

  4. Day 4, Munnar to Thekkady

    Spice plantation walk, then an optional boat ride on Periyar lake or a Kalaripayattu show.

  5. Day 5, Alappuzha houseboat

    Board at noon. Lunch, dinner and the night on a private houseboat on Vembanad lake.

  6. Day 6, Departure

    Breakfast on board, then drop at Kochi airport or Ernakulam station.

Questions people ask

Can you change a package to suit us?

Yes. Every package is a starting point. We can add or remove nights, change hotels, add a day for rest, or plan around elders and small children.

Are train or flight tickets included?

Not in the listed price, because fares change every day. We book them for you at the current fare and add it to your plan. Group departures include them, as written in each trip.

What is your cancellation policy?

More than 30 days before travel, we refund everything except non refundable hotel and ticket charges. Between 30 and 15 days, 25 percent of the package is kept. Less than 15 days, the hotel's own policy applies. You get it in writing before you pay.

Do you arrange vegetarian or Jain food?

Yes. We note it with every hotel and houseboat, and on pilgrimage group trips we travel with South Indian meals.

How do I pay?

UPI, debit or credit card, or bank transfer. You get a GST invoice for every payment.

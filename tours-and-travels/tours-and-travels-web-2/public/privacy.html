Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Dakshin Yatra Tours ("we") handles personal data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

This website does not store anything you type. When you use the enquiry form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing your name, phone number, chosen trip, group size, date and notes. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in WhatsApp.

If you call, message or email us, we receive the details you choose to share.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, plan and book your trip, and share details with hotels, drivers and guides needed for that trip. We do not sell your data or send marketing without your consent.

How long we keep it

Enquiry chats are deleted within 12 months if you do not book. Booking records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. Contact hello@dakshinyatra.in or call +91 98765 43210.

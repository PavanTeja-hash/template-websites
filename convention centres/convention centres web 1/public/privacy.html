Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information GRANDVIEW Convention Centre ("we") collects through www.grandview.in and how we use it. We keep it short because the website collects very little.

What this website stores

  • Nothing about you. The website has no log-in, no database, no advertising cookies and no analytics.
  • Fonts. Text fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, as with any website request.

When you enquire or contact us

The availability form does not send anything to a server. It puts the details you enter (name, mobile number, organisation, event type, guest count, hall, date, number of days and notes) into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. Once you send it, or call, email or message us, we receive those details and use them only to:

  • tell you which halls are free and send you a quote;
  • arrange a site visit and menu tasting;
  • prepare your booking agreement, take payments and issue invoices;
  • plan and run your event, including sharing what is needed with vendors you ask us to coordinate;
  • keep the records we are required to keep by law (for example, invoices for tax purposes).

We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing. If you'd like to hear about offers, we'll ask you first.

WhatsApp

Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under their own privacy policy. We only see what you send in the chat.

Guest information

If you share guest lists, speaker details or exhibitor contacts with us for registration, seating or security, we use them only for your event and delete or return them after it, unless you ask us to keep them or the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into a booking are deleted within 12 months. Booking and invoice records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing events@grandview.in or calling +91 20 6789 1234. We will reply within 30 days. This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

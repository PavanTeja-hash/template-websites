FAQ

Common Questions

What is the maximum capacity of the venue?
The Grand Ballroom seats 1,200 in theatre style and takes up to 2,000 guests in a standing or cocktail layout. For smaller events, the Conference Hall seats up to 600, the Boardroom up to 50 and the Open-Air Lawn up to 800. Final numbers depend on your layout, stage and decor, and we confirm them at the site visit.
Can we bring our own caterer?
We offer in-house catering for veg, non-veg and multi-cuisine menus. Outside caterers are allowed in the Grand Ballroom and on the Open-Air Lawn for a kitchen-usage fee, which is listed in your quote. The Conference Hall and Boardroom use in-house catering only.
Is there parking for guests?
Yes. We have covered multi-level parking for 500+ cars and a separate two-wheeler area. Valet service is included for evening and weekend events, and optional for weekday corporate events at a flat fee.
What AV equipment is available?
All halls come with built-in projectors and screens, wireless microphones, a PA system and stage lighting. The Conference Hall also has simultaneous translation booths and live-streaming equipment. A dedicated AV technician is present for every event.
How far in advance should we book?
Weekends and the wedding season (November to February) fill up 3 to 6 months ahead. Weekday corporate events can often be confirmed 2 to 4 weeks out. Check availability as early as you can, especially for multi-day events.
How is a date confirmed?
An enquiry doesn't hold a date. After the site visit we send a written, itemised quote. Your date is confirmed when you sign the booking agreement and pay the advance shown in the quote. Payment, cancellation and refund terms are set out in that agreement, so please read them before you pay.
Can we use the Open-Air Lawn during the monsoon?
The lawn is open for bookings outside the monsoon, roughly October to May. For lawn events at other times, or if rain is forecast, we can hold an indoor hall as a backup, subject to availability.
Do you handle decor and entertainment?
We have empanelled decorators, florists, DJs and entertainment vendors, and you're free to bring your own. Our event manager coordinates all vendors either way, so you have a single point of contact on the day.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the maximum capacity of the venue?
The Grand Ballroom seats 1,200 in theatre style and accommodates up to 2,000 guests in a floating/cocktail layout. For smaller events, the Conference Hall seats 600, the Boardroom takes 50 and the Open Lawn fits 800.
Can we bring our own caterer?
We offer in-house catering for veg, non-veg and multi-cuisine. Outside caterers are permitted for the Grand Ballroom and Open Lawn at a nominal kitchen-usage fee. The Conference Hall and Boardroom use in-house catering only.
Is there parking for guests?
Yes. We have covered multi-level parking for 500+ cars and a separate two-wheeler area. Valet service is included for evening and weekend events, and optional for weekday corporate events at a flat fee.
What AV equipment is available?
All halls come with built-in projectors and screens, wireless microphones, a PA system and stage lighting. The Conference Hall also has simultaneous translation booths and live-streaming equipment. A dedicated AV technician is present for every event.
How far in advance should we book?
Weekends and wedding season (November-February) fill up 3-6 months ahead. Weekday corporate events can often be confirmed 2-4 weeks out. We recommend checking availability as early as possible, especially for multi-day events.
Do you handle decor and entertainment?
We have empanelled decorators, florists, DJs and entertainment vendors. You're free to bring your own as well. Our event manager coordinates all vendors regardless, so you have a single point of contact on the day.

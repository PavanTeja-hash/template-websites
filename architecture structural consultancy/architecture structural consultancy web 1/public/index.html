Our Process

How a project moves through us

Transparent timelines. No scope creep surprises.

  1. Week 1

    Site Survey & Brief

    Our team visits your site, arranges the soil test and survey, photographs the context, and sits with you to document requirements, budget constraints, and timeline.

  2. Week 2-3

    Concept & 3D Walkthrough

    Two to three concept directions in 3D visualisation. You walk through your building before a single brick is placed. Revisions included until you sign off.

  3. Week 4-6

    Detailed Drawings & Approvals

    Architectural floor plans, structural drawings (RCC/steel), MEP coordination, and building permission submissions to GHMC, HMDA or your local body, prepared and filed by us.

  4. Construction Phase

    Site Supervision & Quality Control

    Our structural engineer visits at every critical milestone: foundation, column casting, slab pour, waterproofing. No deviation goes unnoticed.

FAQ

Questions we get asked every week

What is the difference between an architect and a structural consultant?
An architect designs the look, layout, and spatial experience of a building. A structural consultant calculates the load-bearing framework: foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and retaining walls. At ARCVAULT, both work under one roof so your design intent and structural safety are always aligned.
How much does architectural consultancy cost for a residential project?
Residential consultancy (architecture + structural) typically ranges from Rs 25 to Rs 55 per sq ft of built-up area, depending on complexity. This covers concept design, 3D visualisation, working drawings, structural drawings, municipal approvals, and site supervision at critical milestones. We provide a fixed-fee quote before starting. Statutory fees and taxes are extra.
Do you handle municipal approvals and RERA registration?
We prepare and submit approval drawings to GHMC, HMDA or your local body, respond to scrutiny comments, handle revision submissions, and apply for fire NOC and airport height clearance where required. For multi-unit developments we prepare the architect's and engineer's certificates and drawings your RERA registration needs.
Can you work with my existing contractor?
Absolutely. Many clients hire us for design and structural consultancy while using their own trusted contractor for construction. We provide construction-ready drawings, bar bending schedules, and supervise critical pours to ensure structural integrity regardless of who builds.
How do you ensure structural safety in earthquake-prone areas?
Our structural designs follow IS 456 and IS 1893 (the seismic code) for the applicable zone, with ductile detailing to IS 13920 where required. We use ETABS/STAAD for analysis, specify proper reinforcement lap lengths and anchorages, and every design is reviewed by a senior structural engineer before release.
What is your revision policy during the design phase?
Concept stage: unlimited revisions within the agreed scope. Detailed drawing stage: two rounds of revisions included. Additional revisions beyond that are billed at a nominal hourly rate, quoted in advance. We find that the 3D walkthrough stage eliminates most revision needs because clients see exactly what they are getting before we move to detailing.

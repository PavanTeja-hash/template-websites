Our Approach

What makes working with us different

Six things clients wish they had known before hiring their first architect.

  1. 01

    One firm, both disciplines

    Your architect and structural engineer sit in the same room. A design change at 3 PM is reflected in the structural model by 5 PM. No weeks of back-and-forth between two separate offices.

  2. 02

    Fixed fee, quoted upfront

    We quote a lump-sum fee before work begins. If approvals need extra rounds of revision, we absorb the hours. The scope is written down, so there are no surprise invoices.

  3. 03

    3D walkthrough before you spend a rupee on bricks

    Every client gets a photorealistic 3D walkthrough of their building. See every room, terrace, and facade angle before construction starts. Revisions are free until sign-off.

  4. 04

    Site supervision is not optional

    Our structural engineer is on site at every critical milestone: foundation, column casting, slab pour. Problems get caught before they are cast in concrete.

  5. 05

    BIM coordination catches clashes before they cost money

    Every project runs through Revit BIM. Plumbing through a beam, a duct hitting a lintel: we find and fix it on screen, not on site where it costs far more.

  6. 06

    Vastu-compliant without compromised livability

    We integrate vastu principles into modern floor plans without awkward room shapes or dead corridors. Clients get directional compliance and genuine usability in one layout.

FAQ

Questions we hear every week

What does your consultancy fee include?
Concept design, 3D visualisation, working drawings (architectural + structural), municipal/PMC approval submissions, BIM coordination, bar bending schedules, and site supervision at every critical pour. MEP coordination is included; detailed MEP design is an add-on.
Can I hire you for structural engineering only?
Yes. If you already have an architect, we plug into their workflow and deliver structural design, analysis, and site supervision independently. We work with your architect's drawings in Revit or AutoCAD.
How long does the full design process take?
Typical residential: 6-8 weeks from first meeting to approval-ready drawings. Commercial: 8-14 weeks depending on scale. These timelines include client review rounds and municipal submission. The time the authority takes to grant approval is on top of this.
Do you do interior design as well?
We handle interior architecture: space planning, partition layouts, ceiling plans, material specifications, and electrical/plumbing coordination. For decorative interiors (furniture selection, soft furnishings), we collaborate with partner interior designers.
How do you handle seismic design?
All structural designs follow IS 1893 for earthquake loads, with IS 456 for concrete design and ductile detailing per IS 13920. We use ETABS for multi-storey dynamic analysis and STAAD for industrial structures. Pune is in Zone III; for critical structures we can design to higher seismic factors when the client asks.
What if I want to change the design during construction?
Minor changes (partition shifts, window repositioning) are accommodated at no extra charge. Structural changes (adding a floor, moving columns) require re-analysis and are quoted separately before proceeding. We always advise against structural changes post-foundation.

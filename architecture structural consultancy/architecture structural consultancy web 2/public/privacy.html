Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information STRUCTURA Design + Engineering LLP ("STRUCTURA", "we") collects through www.structura.co.in and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you choose to send us: your name, mobile number, email, project type, approximate built-up area, site location and project brief.
  • Anything you share when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp, such as site photos, plans, soil reports or survey documents.

The project form on this website does not store or send anything to our servers. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device, and nothing reaches us until you send it. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver the design, engineering, approval and supervision services you engage us for.
  • To meet legal, tax and professional record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Sharing

We share project information only where the work needs it, for example with the municipal corporation or planning authority when filing approvals, with soil-testing or survey agencies, or with your contractor when you ask us to. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 12 months. Project records are kept for as long as professional and legal obligations require.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. Write to studio@structura.co.in and we will respond within 30 days.

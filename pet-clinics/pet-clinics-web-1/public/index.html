What we do

Veterinary services

Complete healthcare for dogs, cats, birds and small animals, from first vaccines to senior care.

General consultation

Routine check-ups, illness diagnosis and clear treatment plans from experienced vets.

Vaccinations

Core and lifestyle vaccines for puppies, kittens and adults, with digital vaccination records.

Surgery

Spay and neuter, orthopaedic and soft-tissue procedures with monitored anaesthesia and post-op care.

Diagnostics

In-house blood work, digital X-ray, ultrasound and ECG. Most reports ready within 2 hours.

Emergency care

24/7 emergency line for accidents, poisoning, bites and sudden illness. Call before you travel.

Pet pharmacy

Prescription medicines, supplements, flea and tick prevention and therapeutic diets on site.

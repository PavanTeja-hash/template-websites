Life at Snuggle Stay

Frequently asked questions

Is it really cage-free?

Yes. We have no cages or kennels. Dogs have open play areas during the day and individual sleeping suites at night. Cats have their own enclosed lounge with climbing structures.

What vaccinations are required?

Dogs: Rabies, DHPP (distemper combo) and kennel cough — all within the last 12 months. Cats: Rabies and FVRCP. Please bring vaccination records at check-in.

Can I bring my pet's own food?

Absolutely. We follow your pet's exact diet and feeding schedule. Just pack enough for their stay with feeding instructions.

What happens in a medical emergency?

We have a vet on call 24/7 and a partnered clinic 2 km away. You'll be contacted immediately. Emergency vet costs are billed separately.

Do you accept aggressive or anxious dogs?

We require a temperament assessment (free, 30 minutes) before the first stay. Reactive dogs get solo play sessions instead of group play.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in: 8 am to 11 am. Check-out: 4 pm to 7 pm. Early drop-off or late pick-up can be arranged at ₹100 extra.

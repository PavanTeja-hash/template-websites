FAQ

Common Questions

How many photos will we receive?
It depends on the package. A half-day shoot delivers 200+ edited photos, a full wedding 400+, and a multi-day package 600+. Every photo is colour-graded and retouched. We deliver full-resolution files via a private online gallery you can share with family.
Do you travel for destination weddings?
Yes. We've shot weddings across Rajasthan, Goa, Himachal and Kerala. Travel and stay costs are additional but we keep them transparent. We share a detailed travel estimate before you confirm.
How long before we get the photos and video?
Edited photos are delivered in 15-30 working days depending on the package. The cinematic highlight film takes 20-30 days. Same-day teasers (1-min Instagram reels) are delivered within 24 hours of the event.
What equipment do you use?
We shoot on full-frame mirrorless bodies with professional prime and zoom lenses. Video is captured in 4K on gimbals, with off-camera lighting and a drone for aerials where venue rules allow. We always bring backup bodies and cards to every shoot.
Can we get both traditional and candid coverage?
Absolutely. Our crew works in pairs. One photographer handles the posed family portraits and group shots. The other moves through the event capturing candid moments, emotions and details. You get both styles in one package.
What's the booking process and payment terms?
We start with a 15-minute call to understand your event. After that we share a detailed quote. A 30% advance confirms your date. 50% is due a week before the event, and the remaining 20% on delivery. We share a simple contract that covers deliverables, timelines and cancellation terms.

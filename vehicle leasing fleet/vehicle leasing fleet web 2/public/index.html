FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum subscription size?
3 vehicles for standard subscriptions, 2 for EV-only plans. Volume-tier pricing kicks in at 10 vehicles, with deeper discounts at 50 and 100+.
What exactly is included in the monthly fee?
The vehicle, comprehensive insurance, scheduled maintenance, tyre and battery replacements, 24/7 roadside assistance, telematics hardware and dashboard, and a vehicle replacement guarantee. You pay only fuel and tolls.
Can I scale up or down during the contract?
Yes. Scale-up requests are fulfilled within 48 hours. Scale-down requires 30 days' notice, with no early-exit penalty on individual vehicles after the first 6 months.
How does EV transition work?
We assess your routes for EV suitability, help with the paperwork for any central and state EV incentives you qualify for, plan charging at your facilities, and gradually swap ICE vehicles for EVs over 12-24 months, without upfront capital.
What happens at the end of the subscription?
Three options: renew with fresh vehicles at a refreshed rate, extend the current subscription at a reduced rate, or return the fleet. No residual value risk, no depreciation headaches.
What telematics data can I access?
Real-time GPS, fuel consumption analytics, driver behaviour scoring (harsh braking, speeding, idling), geofencing alerts, predictive maintenance schedules, and monthly utilization reports. All on one web dashboard and mobile app.

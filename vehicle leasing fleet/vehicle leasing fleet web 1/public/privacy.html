Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how VELO FLEET Pvt. Ltd. ("VELO FLEET", "we") handles information when you use www.velofleet.in.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts, does not use cookies for tracking and does not store the details you type into the fleet audit form. When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. The details reach us only if you choose to send that message.

The lease calculator runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent anywhere.

Information you send us

If you contact us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, we receive what you share, such as your name, company, work email, mobile number, fleet size and requirements. We use it only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a fleet audit or lease proposal, and manage any contract that follows.

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We share it only with partners needed to deliver your lease (for example insurers, financiers and service centres), and only after you engage us.
  • We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

Messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data you have shared with us, or withdraw your consent. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

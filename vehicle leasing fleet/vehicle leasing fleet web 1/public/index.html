FAQ

Questions fleet managers ask

What is the minimum fleet size for a lease contract?
We start at 5 vehicles for standard leases and 3 vehicles for EV-only contracts. Each additional vehicle benefits from volume-tier pricing.
Is the lease truly all-inclusive?
Yes. Every lease covers scheduled maintenance, comprehensive insurance, 24/7 roadside assistance, tyre and battery replacements, and a vehicle replacement guarantee. Fuel and tolls are the only variable costs on your end.
How does the EV transition programme work?
We assess your current fleet utilisation, identify routes suited to EVs, help with central and state EV incentive applications, install charging infrastructure and gradually swap ICE vehicles for EVs over 12-24 months. No upfront capital is required.
Can I add or remove vehicles mid-contract?
Yes. Scale-up requests are fulfilled within 48 hours. Scale-down follows a 30-day notice period, with no early termination penalty on individual vehicles after the first 6 months.
What telematics data do I get access to?
Real-time GPS, fuel consumption, driver behaviour scoring (harsh braking, speeding, idle time), geofencing alerts, predictive maintenance schedules and monthly fleet utilisation reports, all on a single web dashboard and mobile app.
What happens at the end of the lease term?
Three options: renew with refreshed vehicles, extend the existing lease at a reduced rate, or return the fleet. No residual value risk and no depreciation headaches.

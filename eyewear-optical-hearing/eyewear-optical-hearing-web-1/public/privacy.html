Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Aura Optics (“we”, “us”) runs this website to tell you about our frames, lenses and eye care services and to let you request an appointment. This page explains what information we handle and why.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts, does not use cookies for tracking and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The booking form does not send anything to a server. When you press “Request on WhatsApp”, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing the details you typed (name, phone number, optional email, visit type, preferred date and time). Nothing is shared until you choose to send that message.

How we use your details

  • To confirm, reschedule or remind you about your appointment.
  • To tell you when your glasses or contact lenses are ready.
  • To keep a record of your prescription and eye exam results, which helps us care for your eyes on later visits.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Eye health records

Prescriptions and exam results recorded at the store are kept securely and are only seen by our optometrists and staff who need them to serve you. You can ask for a copy of your prescription at any time.

Third-party services

Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp’s privacy policy. Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you. Call +91 40 2345 6789 or email hello@auraoptics.in, and we will respond within 30 days.

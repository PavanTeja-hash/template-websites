What you're really wondering

We hear these concerns every day. Here's the honest answer.

"Are bespoke frames worth the price?"

Our frames last 3-5x longer than mass-market pairs. Titanium hinges, hand-polished acetate and a 2-year warranty mean you buy fewer frames over time.

"How long will it take to get my glasses?"

Single-vision lenses are ready in 60 minutes. Progressives and specialty coatings take 3-5 business days. We'll call you the moment they're done.

"Is an eye exam really necessary every year?"

Absolutely. Conditions like glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy show no symptoms early on. Our comprehensive exam catches issues well before you notice them.

"Will blue-light glasses actually help?"

Our blue-cut lenses filter 40% of HEV light. Customers report less eye strain and better sleep. No yellow tint: we use a clear-coat filter that keeps colours true.

Comprehensive eye exams

Common questions

Do you accept insurance or corporate plans?

We work with most major vision insurance providers and several corporate wellness programmes. Bring your card and we will check coverage on the spot.

Can I get my lenses replaced in an existing frame?

Yes. If your frame is structurally sound, we can reglaze it with new lenses. Bring the frame in and we will measure it. Reglazing starts at ₹1,200.

Do you offer home eye exams?

We offer home visits for senior citizens and patients with mobility issues within a 15 km radius. Call us to schedule a home exam slot.

What brands do you stock?

We carry a mix of international designer frames and our own Aura Optics in-house line. If you have a specific brand in mind, we can special-order it in 7-10 days.

How often should I replace my glasses?

We recommend an eye exam every 12 months. If your prescription changes, new lenses are essential. Frames typically last 2-4 years with proper care.

Do you do contact lens fittings?

Yes. Our optometrists fit soft, toric and multifocal contacts. Trial lenses are provided at no extra charge so you can test comfort before ordering.

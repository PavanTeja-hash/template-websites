Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I need a hearing aid?

If you frequently ask people to repeat themselves, struggle in noisy environments, or find yourself turning up the TV louder than others prefer, a hearing test can tell you exactly where you stand. The test is free and takes just 30 minutes.

How long do hearing aids last?

Most modern digital hearing aids last 4-6 years with proper care. We provide free cleaning and maintenance throughout the life of your device. Battery life for rechargeable models is typically 18-24 hours per charge.

Will people notice I'm wearing a hearing aid?

CIC and RIC styles are virtually invisible. Even BTE models today are sleek and compact. Many of our patients report that friends and family don't notice their hearing aids at all.

Do you offer a trial period?

Yes. Every hearing aid comes with a trial period so you can test it in your daily life. If it doesn't meet your expectations, we'll adjust the settings, try a different model, or offer a full refund.

Can I connect my hearing aid to my phone?

Most of our hearing aids support Bluetooth connectivity. You can stream phone calls, music, podcasts, and even TV audio directly to your hearing aids from your smartphone.

What brands do you carry?

We are authorised partners of Phonak, Signia (Siemens), Oticon, Widex, Starkey, and Resound. This means we can match you with the best device regardless of brand, rather than pushing a single manufacturer.

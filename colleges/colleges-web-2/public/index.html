About Prashanti

Founded in 1994, Prashanti Institute of Technology is one of Bengaluru's leading engineering colleges. Spread across a 25-acre campus in Whitefield, we offer world-class infrastructure including advanced research labs, a digital library, an innovation hub and a 1,200-seat auditorium.

Our industry tie-ups with leading IT companies ensure students graduate with practical skills and strong placement support. The training and placement cell has consistently placed 90%+ students in companies like Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Accenture and startups.

Admissions 2026-27

Admission through KCET / COMEDK / Management quota. Applications open now.

1

Check eligibility

Minimum 45% in PUC / 12th (40% for reserved categories). Valid KCET or COMEDK rank.

2

Submit documents

SSLC, PUC marks card, KCET/COMEDK rank card, transfer certificate, caste certificate if applicable.

3

Pay fees and enrol

Complete fee payment at the accounts office. Collect ID card, hostel allotment and class schedule.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Vidyasagar College of Arts and Science ("we") handles personal data through this website, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the form opens your own email app with your name, email and message filled in. Nothing is stored on this website; we only receive what you choose to send by email.
  • Results and parent portal fields: the register number you type is checked in your browser only and is not sent or stored.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider may keep standard logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) for security, for a limited period.

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and handle admissions, examination or fee matters you raise. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by writing to our Grievance Officer at info@vidyasagarcollege.edu.in or calling +91 80 2345 6789. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

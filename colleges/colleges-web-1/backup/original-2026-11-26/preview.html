About Vidyasagar

Established in 1987, Vidyasagar College of Arts and Science has been a trusted institution in Bengaluru for over three decades. Our campus spans 12 acres in Rajajinagar with well-equipped labs, a central library with 45,000 volumes, smart classrooms and separate hostels for men and women.

We believe in holistic education that prepares students not just for exams but for life. Our faculty-to-student ratio of 1:20 ensures personalised attention in every classroom.

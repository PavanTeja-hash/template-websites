FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the things that hold most people back from booking.

Do I need to provide cleaning equipment or products?
No. Our team arrives with vacuum cleaners, mops, microfibre cloths and eco-friendly cleaning agents. You do not need to provide anything.
How do you verify your cleaning staff?
Every SPARKHAUS cleaner goes through police verification, a government photo ID check and an in-person interview. We keep emergency contact details and a photo ID on file for every staff member.
What if something is damaged during cleaning?
Every booking is covered by our damage protection policy. If an item is accidentally damaged during the visit, we pay for the repair or replacement up to Rs 10,000. Just report it within 24 hours.
Can I book the same cleaner every time?
Yes. On a weekly or bi-weekly plan we assign the same cleaner (or team) to your home, so they learn your preferences and your home over time.
Are your products safe for children and pets?
We use plant-based cleaning agents without ammonia, chlorine or synthetic fragrances, chosen to be gentle for homes with children, pets and allergy-sensitive people. Tell us about any allergies when you book.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
You can reschedule or cancel for free up to 4 hours before the appointment. Cancellations within 4 hours may have a Rs 200 fee to cover the cleaner's travel. Regular plans can be paused for up to 2 weeks at no charge.

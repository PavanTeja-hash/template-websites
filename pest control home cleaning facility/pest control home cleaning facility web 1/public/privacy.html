Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how PESTSHIELD Pest Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us") handles personal information shared through www.pestshield.in.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts and does not store form entries. The booking form checks your details in your browser and then opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in WhatsApp.

What we receive

When you message, call or email us, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, mobile number, email, locality, property type, pest problem and preferred date.

How we use it

  • To schedule and carry out your inspection and treatment.
  • To issue invoices, warranty cards and, for businesses, service reports.
  • To remind you about follow-up visits and warranty checks.

We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Third parties

WhatsApp messages are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

How long we keep it

We keep service records for as long as your warranty lasts and as required by tax and pest control record-keeping rules, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Contact us at help@pestshield.in or +91 80 4567 1234.

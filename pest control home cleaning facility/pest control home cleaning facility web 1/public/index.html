FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Is the treatment safe for children and pets?
We use CIB&RC-registered, low-toxicity products at label doses. Gel baits go in hidden spots (behind appliances, inside cabinet corners) that children and pets can't reach. For spray treatments, keep children and pets out of the treated room for about 2 hours, then ventilate. The technician tells you the exact re-entry time before starting.
Do I need to empty my kitchen cabinets before treatment?
For cockroach gel baiting, no. The gel goes in tiny dots behind and under shelves. For spray treatments in the kitchen, please cover or put away open food. Sealed containers and packaged food can stay. The technician will brief you before starting.
How long does a typical treatment take?
Cockroach or ant gel: 30 to 45 minutes for a 2 BHK. Termite (post-construction): 3 to 5 hours depending on area. Bed bugs: 2 to 3 hours per room. Rodent control: about 45 minutes to set up, plus 2 follow-up visits over 2 weeks.
What does the warranty cover?
If the treated pest comes back within the warranty period, we re-treat at no extra cost. The terms are printed on your warranty card. Termite: 5 years. Cockroach: 3 months per treatment (12 months on the Annual Care Plan). Bed bugs: 90 days. Rodents: 60 days, with entry-point sealing. Wood borer: 1 year.
Do you provide service reports for FSSAI audits?
Yes. Commercial clients get a pest management record after every visit: pests seen, treatment applied, product used (with batch number), areas treated and technician details. We can also share copies of our licence for your records.
What if pests come back after treatment?
Call us. Within the warranty period, we re-treat free. If it's a different pest, we inspect free and quote that treatment separately. The Annual Care Plan covers common household pests with free call-outs between scheduled visits.

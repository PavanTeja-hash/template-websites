Our Process

Five Steps to Flawless Protection

Every car follows the same studio protocol, whether it's a hatchback or a supercar.

  1. 1

    Inspect

    LED light inspection. We photograph swirls, chips and defects and share the report with you.

  2. 2

    Decontaminate

    Hand wash, iron fallout removal and clay bar. The paint must be perfectly clean before correction.

  3. 3

    Correct

    Machine compounding in stages to remove swirls and restore depth. We check under LED after every pass.

  4. 4

    Coat

    Ceramic applied panel by panel in an enclosed bay. Each layer cures under infrared lamps before the next goes on.

  5. 5

    Cure & Deliver

    24-hour controlled cure. Final inspection, a walk-around with you and a care kit for home maintenance.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does ceramic coating last?
It depends on the package: up to 3 years for Essential, 5 years for Pro and 7 years for Ultimate. You get a coating card with the date, products and layers applied. Real-world life also depends on washing habits, parking (covered or open) and whether you follow the care guide we give you.
Is ceramic coating worth it on a new car?
Yes. New paint is in its best condition, so coating it before swirls and scratches build up needs the least correction and gives the best result. Even new cars often pick up wash marks at the dealer, so we still inspect and lightly correct before coating.
What's the difference between ceramic coating and PPF?
Ceramic coating is a thin liquid layer that bonds to the paint. It adds gloss, water repellency and chemical resistance, but it doesn't stop physical impacts. PPF (paint protection film) is a thick, self-healing urethane film that physically blocks stone chips and light scratches. Our Ultimate package puts film on the front, where most chips happen, and ceramic over everything.
Can I wash my car normally after coating?
Yes, after the first 7 days. Use the two-bucket method or a foam cannon with a pH-neutral shampoo, and avoid automatic brush car washes. The care kit we give you has what you need for the first few months.
Do you offer pickup and drop?
Yes. Pickup and drop within Hyderabad is free with the Pro and Ultimate packages. For other services it's Rs 500 each way. Tell us your area when you book.
How long does the process take?
Essential takes about 2 days, Pro 2 to 3 days, and Ultimate (front PPF + ceramic) 4 to 5 days. That includes correction, coating and curing. We don't rush the cure, and we send you photo updates on WhatsApp while your car is with us.
How do I pay?
The inspection is free and you get a fixed quote before any work starts. You pay at handover by UPI, card or bank transfer once you're happy with the car.

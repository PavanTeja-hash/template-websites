Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information CERAMIQ ("we") collects through www.ceramiq.in and how we use it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What this website stores

  • Nothing about you. The website has no log-in, no database, no advertising cookies and no analytics.
  • Fonts. Text fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, as with any website request.

When you book or contact us

The booking form does not send anything to a server. It puts the details you enter (name, mobile number, car, service, preferred date, area and notes) into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. Once you send it, or call, email or message us, we receive those details and use them only to:

  • confirm and schedule your appointment, and arrange pickup and drop if you ask for it;
  • send you photo updates and the inspection report for your car;
  • take payment, issue an invoice and handle questions about the work;
  • remind you about maintenance visits included in your package;
  • keep the records we are required to keep by law (for example, invoices for tax purposes).

Photos we take of your car are used for your inspection report. We only post them on social media or this website if you agree, and we hide the number plate.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask us to. WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep invoices and job records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax, and chat history only as long as we need it to serve you and honour your package's maintenance visits.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to what we must keep by law. Contact us using the details below.

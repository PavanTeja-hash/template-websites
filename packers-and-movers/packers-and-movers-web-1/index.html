We know what worries you

Every person moving has the same fears. Here is how we solve each one.

"Will my stuff get damaged?"

Your biggest fear

We use 5-ply cartons, 40 GSM bubble wrap, foam corners and wooden crating for fragile items. Every piece is barcoded and photographed before loading. If anything is damaged, our full-value insurance covers repair or replacement at market value within 15 days.

"Will they add hidden charges?"

The surprise bill anxiety

Our move manager visits your home, counts every item and gives you a written, all-inclusive quote. That number is the number. No toll charges, no packing-material surcharges, no "oh this costs extra" on moving day. We put it in the contract.

"Will they show up on time?"

The waiting game

Your move manager shares their live location 30 minutes before arrival. If we are late by more than 1 hour, you get 10% off your bill. No questions asked. We have maintained a 97% on-time record across 12,000+ moves.

"Where is my truck right now?"

The anxiety of not knowing

Every truck has GPS. After loading, you get a live tracking link on WhatsApp. Watch your belongings move in real time and see the truck location, speed and ETA any time. We also send automatic updates at checkpoints.

"Can I trust the workers in my home?"

Strangers around your belongings

Every crew member is background-verified and ID-carded. They wear uniforms, use shoe covers indoors and work under a team leader you have already met during the survey. We have been doing this since 2015 with zero safety incidents.

"What if something goes missing?"

The inventory nightmare

We barcode and photograph every single item before packing. You get a digital inventory list. At delivery, we tick off each item in front of you. If anything is missing, the claim process starts immediately.

Still have questions?

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 7-10 days. For peak season (April-June), 2-3 weeks. Last-minute bookings are accommodated when trucks are available.

Is my shipment insured?

Basic transit insurance is included free. Full-value insurance (covers repair or replacement at current market value) is available at 2-3% of declared value.

Can I track my shipment?

Every truck is GPS-enabled. You will receive a tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp after loading, with real-time location and ETA updates.

Do you move cars and two-wheelers?

Yes. Cars in enclosed carriers, two-wheelers on custom frames. Both include door-step pickup and delivery.

What if something gets damaged?

File a claim within 48 hours. We document everything with photographs at both ends. Insured items are repaired or replaced within 15 working days.

Can I store items temporarily?

Yes. Our climate-controlled warehouses offer flexible storage from 1 week to 12 months with 24/7 CCTV and pest control.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a same-day move?

Yes! If you book before 12 PM, we can usually dispatch a crew the same afternoon. After 12 PM, we will confirm availability within 30 minutes.

What is included in the flat rate?

Packing material (cartons, bubble wrap, tape), loading, transport, unloading and basic arrangement. Furniture disassembly/assembly is included in the 1-2 BHK and 3-4 BHK packages.

Do you charge for floors?

If the building has a working lift, there is no floor charge. Without a lift, we charge ₹100 per floor at both pickup and delivery addresses.

Can I move just one or two items?

Absolutely. Single-item moves (fridge, washing machine, sofa, bed) start at ₹800. Tell us what and where, and we will quote instantly on WhatsApp.

Is there insurance?

Basic transit cover is included free in all packages. Full-value insurance is available at 2% of declared value for high-value items.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer and credit/debit cards. Payment is collected after delivery, not before.

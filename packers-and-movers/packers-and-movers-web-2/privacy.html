Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

QuickHaul Local Movers (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. When you use the booking form, the details you enter (your name, phone number, pickup and drop areas, date, package and notes) are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent to us until you choose to send that message in WhatsApp.

How we use it

We use the details you send only to quote your move, schedule the crew, carry out the move and handle any insurance claim. We never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Move records

Addresses, item lists and photos collected for your move are shared only with you, the crew assigned to your move and, if you make a claim, our insurance partner.

Third-party services

Messages you send on WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Call +91 81234 56789 or email book@quickhaul.in.

FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should we book?
For large conferences and multi-day events, 3-6 months is ideal. Product launches and award nights need 6-8 weeks minimum. Town halls and smaller activations can be turned around in 3-4 weeks if venue availability permits.
Do you work outside Mumbai?
Yes. We have on-ground teams and vendor networks in 18 cities including Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Goa, Jaipur and Kolkata. For international events, we partner with vetted local production houses.
Can we hire you for AV and production only?
Absolutely. Many clients bring us in specifically for stage design, sound, LED, lighting and live streaming while managing the rest in-house. We're flexible on scope.
What does a typical event cost?
It depends on format, scale and production complexity. A 200-person town hall starts around Rs 4-6 lakh. A 1,000+ delegate conference with full production is typically Rs 25-50 lakh. We share an itemized budget after the discovery call so you see exactly where every rupee goes.
Do you handle travel and hotel bookings?
Yes. For dealer meets, offsites and multi-city events, we manage flights, hotel blocks, airport transfers and on-ground logistics. Our travel desk handles groups of 50 to 2,000.
What industries do you work with?
IT, BFSI, pharma, automotive, FMCG, real estate and startups. We've worked with Series A companies doing their first offsite and with Fortune 500 firms running 5,000-person annual conferences.
